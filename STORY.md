# Story bible

The stories share one recurring cast. This file keeps them consistent: read "Where the story stands" and "Continuity facts" before planning or writing a unit, check the new stories against the cast, places and timeline, and update it after each unit (CLAUDE.md › Building a unit, step 9).

Lesson ids in brackets show where a fact is stated. "Implied" means the stories suggest it without saying it. The grammar lessons' texts about sounds (u03-sounds, u05-nasals, u08-u-ou, u16-r) are standalone and don't use the cast.
